FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our environmental services and MPCB compliance.

How do I know if my industry needs MPCB consent?
Industries are classified as Red, Orange, Green or White based on their pollution potential. Red, Orange and Green category units need Consent to Establish before setting up and Consent to Operate before starting production. White category units are generally exempt from consent but may need to inform MPCB. We offer a free category check for your unit.
What are the key requirements for ETP / STP compliance?
Treated water must meet the parameters in your consent (such as pH, BOD, COD, TSS and oil & grease), monitoring must be done at the required frequency, and logbooks of flow, power, chemicals and sludge must be maintained. Annual returns and the Environmental Statement must also be filed on time.
How do online monitoring systems (OCEMS) work?
Online continuous emission and effluent monitoring systems use analysers to measure stack emissions and treated effluent parameters in real time and transmit the data to the CPCB and MPCB servers. Proper installation, calibration and data connectivity are essential to avoid gaps and false alerts.
Do you offer a single contract for design, approvals and operation?
Yes. Many clients engage us for the complete cycle: approvals, design and installation of treatment systems, testing, annual reporting and operation & maintenance.
