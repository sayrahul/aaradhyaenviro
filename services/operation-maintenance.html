Operation & Maintenance (O&M)

A well-built plant still fails without good operation. Our O&M teams keep your treatment systems running within norms every day.

Get a free consultation +91 99759 29212

Overview

Biological treatment plants need daily attention: correct aeration, sludge wasting, nutrient and chemical dosing, and quick action when the influent changes. Without trained operators and proper records, plants drift out of compliance and equipment breaks down early.

Our O&M service provides trained operators or supervises your staff, maintains daily logbooks, tests key parameters, manages chemical consumption and carries out preventive maintenance of pumps, blowers and filters. You receive regular performance reports, and our engineers step in quickly when problems appear.

Who needs this service

  • Industries with ETP, RO or ZLD plants
  • Housing societies, hospitals, hotels and commercial buildings with STPs
  • Plants with frequent breakdowns or unstable treated-water quality
  • Owners who want one accountable partner for plant performance

What we cover

Plant operation

Trained operators or supervision of your operators, shift-wise.

Process control

MLSS, DO, sludge age, dosing and backwash management.

In-house testing

Routine checks of pH, TDS, MLSS, settleability and other parameters.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled maintenance of pumps, blowers, diffusers, filters and membranes.

Chemicals & consumables

Consumption tracking and timely replenishment.

Reporting

Daily logs and periodic performance reports.

FAQ

Operation & Maintenance (O&M): frequently asked questions

Do you provide operators for STPs in housing societies?
Yes. We provide trained operators and supervision for STPs in housing societies, hospitals, hotels and commercial buildings, along with logbooks and testing.
Can you take over a plant built by another company?
Yes. We start with a takeover audit, list repairs or improvements needed, and then operate the plant under an O&M contract.
What records should a treatment plant maintain?
Typical records include daily flow, power and chemical consumption, operating parameters, test results, sludge disposal, maintenance and breakdown logs.
