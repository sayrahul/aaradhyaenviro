About Aaradhya Enviro

An environmental consultancy and turnkey pollution control company from Waluj MIDC, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, helping industries balance production with environmental responsibility.

Get a free consultation +91 99759 29212

Who we are

Compliance consultants who also build the plants

Environmental pollution is one of the most pressing challenges facing industry today, threatening the air we breathe, the water we drink and the food we grow. As regulations tighten, industries are expected to operate responsibly while remaining profitable.

Aaradhya Enviro was founded in 2018 to solve exactly this problem. Backed by a team with 10+ years of hands-on experience, we have grown from a local compliance consultancy into a full-spectrum environmental solutions provider, serving 500+ clients — from small manufacturing units to large industrial groups, sugar factories, construction companies and multinational corporations.

What sets us apart is that we do not stop at paperwork. We design, install, operate and maintain the physical systems — ETPs, STPs, RO and ZLD plants, air pollution control equipment — that keep a facility compliant every single day. You get one accountable partner for your environmental obligations instead of several vendors.
