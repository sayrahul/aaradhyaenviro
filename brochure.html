1. About us

Aaradhya Enviro is an environmental consultancy and turnkey pollution control company based in Waluj MIDC, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra. Founded in 2018 and backed by a team with 10+ years of experience, we have served 500+ clients, including manufacturing units, industrial groups, sugar factories, construction companies and multinational corporations meet their environmental obligations.

We do not stop at paperwork. We design, install, operate and maintain the physical systems — ETPs, STPs, RO and ZLD plants, air pollution control equipment — that keep a facility compliant every day. Clients get one accountable partner for environment, health and safety (EHS) compliance.

Mission

To make environmental compliance simple, transparent and achievable — protecting the environment while helping clients reduce risk and cost.

Vision

To be one of Maharashtra's most trusted names in environmental consultancy and turnkey pollution control.

Our values

  • Environment first. Every solution is designed to protect air, water and soil, not just to pass an inspection.
  • Client-centric support. Timely, responsive service from the first site visit to years of ongoing O&M.
  • Engineering quality. Designs based on real data, proven technology and careful execution.
  • Trust & transparency. Clear scope, honest timelines and documentation you can rely on.

One partner, every stage

Site assessment → design & engineering → regulatory approvals → fabrication & installation → testing & commissioning → O&M, monitoring and renewals.

2. Our services

Compliance & Consultancy

  • MPCB Consent & Legal Compliance

    Consent to Establish (CTE), Consent to Operate (CTO), renewals, amendments and statutory filings with MPCB.

  • Environmental Clearance (EC)

    EC under the EIA Notification 2006: Form 1, ToR, EIA coordination, public hearing and SEIAA / MoEFCC approvals.

  • Environmental Audit & Reporting

    Environmental Statement (Form V), annual returns, compliance audits and due-diligence reports.

  • CGWA NOC & Bio-Medical Waste Authorisation

    Groundwater extraction NOC (CGWA) and Bio-Medical Waste authorisation for healthcare facilities.

Turnkey Projects

  • ETP & STP Design and Installation

    Turnkey Effluent and Sewage Treatment Plants: design, supply, installation, commissioning and handover.

  • ZLD & RO Systems

    Zero Liquid Discharge and industrial RO: water recovery with UF, RO, evaporators and dryers.

  • Air Pollution Control Systems

    Wet scrubbers, bag filters, cyclones, stacks and ducting to meet emission limits.

  • Plant Up-gradation & Retrofit

    Performance audits, capacity enhancement and retrofits for under-performing treatment plants.

Testing & Monitoring

  • Air, Water & Soil Testing

    Stack, ambient air, effluent, drinking water and soil testing with compliance interpretation.

  • Noise Level Monitoring

    Ambient, boundary, workplace and DG-set noise monitoring with control recommendations.

  • Online Monitoring Systems (OCEMS)

    Online continuous emission and effluent monitoring connected to CPCB / MPCB servers.

  • Treatability Studies

    Lab and pilot trials that prove the right treatment process before you invest.

Management & Training

  • Hazardous Waste Management

    Authorisation, storage, manifests, records and annual returns under the Hazardous Waste Rules.

  • Plastic EPR Compliance

    CPCB EPR registration, targets, certificates and annual returns for plastic packaging.

  • Operation & Maintenance (O&M)

    Trained operators, daily logs, testing and preventive maintenance for ETP, STP, RO and ZLD plants.

  • Compliance Training

    Practical training for managers, EHS teams and plant operators on compliance and plant operation.

  • Nucleonic Gauge & Radioactive Source Compliance

    AERB documentation and coordination for safe, authorised disposal of disused nucleonic sources.

3. Systems we deliver

Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)

Custom-designed turnkey effluent treatment plants that treat industrial wastewater to the limits in your MPCB consent.

Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

Efficient sewage treatment plants (MBBR, SBR, MBR) for safe disposal or reuse of domestic wastewater.

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) System

Integrated UF, RO, evaporator and dryer systems that recover wastewater for reuse and eliminate liquid discharge.

Reverse Osmosis (RO) System

Industrial RO plants for process water, boiler feed water and treated-effluent recovery.

Water Treatment Plant (WTP)

Raw water treatment with filtration, softening and disinfection for industrial, commercial and domestic use.

Air Pollution Control System

Wet scrubbers, bag filters, cyclones and stacks designed, fabricated and erected to meet emission limits.
