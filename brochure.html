01 · Who we are

About Aaradhya Enviro

Environmental pollution is one of the most pressing challenges facing industry today, threatening the air we breathe, the water we drink and the food we grow. As regulations tighten, industries are expected to operate responsibly while staying profitable.

Aaradhya Enviro was founded in 2018 in Waluj MIDC, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, to solve exactly this problem. Backed by a team with 10+ years of hands-on experience, we have served 500+ clients — from small manufacturing units to large industrial groups, sugar factories, construction companies and multinational corporations.

We do not stop at paperwork. We design, install, operate and maintain the physical systems — ETPs, STPs, RO and ZLD plants, air pollution control equipment — that keep a facility compliant every single day.

03 · Compliance & turnkey projects

Our services

Compliance & Consultancy

  • MPCB Consent & Legal Compliance

    Consent to Establish (CTE), Consent to Operate (CTO), renewals, amendments and statutory filings with MPCB.

  • Environmental Clearance (EC)

    EC under the EIA Notification 2006: Form 1, ToR, EIA coordination, public hearing and SEIAA / MoEFCC approvals.

  • Environmental Audit & Reporting

    Environmental Statement (Form V), annual returns, compliance audits and due-diligence reports.

  • CGWA NOC & Bio-Medical Waste Authorisation

    Groundwater extraction NOC (CGWA) and Bio-Medical Waste authorisation for healthcare facilities.

Turnkey Projects

  • ETP & STP Design and Installation

    Turnkey Effluent and Sewage Treatment Plants: design, supply, installation, commissioning and handover.

  • ZLD & RO Systems

    Zero Liquid Discharge and industrial RO: water recovery with UF, RO, evaporators and dryers.

  • Air Pollution Control Systems

    Wet scrubbers, bag filters, cyclones, stacks and ducting to meet emission limits.

  • Plant Up-gradation & Retrofit

    Performance audits, capacity enhancement and retrofits for under-performing treatment plants.

From consent application to commissioned plant

We prepare MPCB and Environmental Clearance applications and design, build and commission the treatment systems those approvals require — so documents and plant performance always match.

04 · Testing, monitoring & management

Our services

Testing & Monitoring

  • Air, Water & Soil Testing

    Stack, ambient air, effluent, drinking water and soil testing with compliance interpretation.

  • Noise Level Monitoring

    Ambient, boundary, workplace and DG-set noise monitoring with control recommendations.

  • Online Monitoring Systems (OCEMS)

    Online continuous emission and effluent monitoring connected to CPCB / MPCB servers.

  • Treatability Studies

    Lab and pilot trials that prove the right treatment process before you invest.

Management & Training

  • Hazardous Waste Management

    Authorisation, storage, manifests, records and annual returns under the Hazardous Waste Rules.

  • Plastic EPR Compliance

    CPCB EPR registration, targets, certificates and annual returns for plastic packaging.

  • Operation & Maintenance (O&M)

    Trained operators, daily logs, testing and preventive maintenance for ETP, STP, RO and ZLD plants.

  • Compliance Training

    Practical training for managers, EHS teams and plant operators on compliance and plant operation.

  • Nucleonic Gauge & Radioactive Source Compliance

    AERB documentation and coordination for safe, authorised disposal of disused nucleonic sources.
