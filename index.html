FAQ

Common questions about environmental compliance

Quick answers on MPCB consents, treatment plants and clearances in Maharashtra.

What are Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO) from MPCB?
CTE is the permission required from the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board before setting up or expanding an industry or project that may cause pollution. CTO is required after construction, before operations start. Both are issued under the Water Act, 1974 and the Air Act, 1981. Operating without valid consent can lead to closure directions and penalties. Aaradhya Enviro handles the full application process, from documentation to MPCB follow-up.
What is the difference between an ETP and an STP?
An Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) treats industrial wastewater containing chemicals, oil, heavy metals or other pollutants. A Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) treats domestic wastewater from toilets, kitchens and washrooms. Factories usually need an ETP; housing societies, hospitals, hotels and commercial buildings need an STP; many industrial sites need both.
How long does Environmental Clearance (EC) take in Maharashtra?
Category B projects appraised by SEIAA Maharashtra commonly take a few months, covering documentation, studies and committee appraisal. Category A projects appraised by MoEFCC usually take longer, especially where a public hearing is required. Correct, complete documentation from the start is the biggest factor in avoiding delays.
What is ZLD (Zero Liquid Discharge) and is it mandatory?
ZLD means all wastewater is treated and recovered for reuse, with no liquid effluent discharged outside the premises. It is required for certain highly polluting sectors and is often written as a condition in consents and Environmental Clearances. Even where it is not mandatory, ZLD can significantly cut fresh water costs.
How much does an ETP or STP cost?
Cost depends on the flow (KLD), pollutant load, required treated-water quality, technology and civil scope. As a rough guide, small plants of 10–50 KLD often start in the range of ₹5–15 lakh, while large industrial plants can cost much more.
